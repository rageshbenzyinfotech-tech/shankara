02 — OUR ARTS

Two Classical
Traditions, One School

We offer authentic training in two of India's most revered classical dance forms — each with its own soul, rhythm and grace.

Bharatanatyam
South Indian Classical

Bharatanatyam

A sacred art originating from the temple traditions of Tamil Nadu, Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest and most elaborate forms of Indian classical dance. It weaves together Nritta (pure dance), Nritya (expressive dance) and Natya (drama) into a single luminous art form.

  • Adavu — Foundational footwork, posture and body alignment
  • Abhinaya — Emotional expression and Navarasas storytelling
  • Hasta Mudras — Classical hand gestures and their meanings
  • Nattuvangam & Rhythm — Tala, mridangam and musicality
  • Arangetram — Preparation for solo debut performance
  • Certificate Course — Recognised certification for dedicated students

Mohiniyattam
Kerala Classical

Mohiniyattam

Originating from Kerala, Mohiniyattam is the dance of the divine enchantress — lyrical, graceful and deeply feminine. Known for its gentle, swaying movements and serene expression, it is a timeless art that embodies the beauty of devotion and grace.

  • Cholkettu — Foundation exercises in body movement and posture
  • Lasya Movement — The lyrical, flowing style unique to Mohiniyattam
  • Abhinaya & Mudras — Expressive gestures rooted in the Hastalakshana Deepika
  • Padam & Keerthanam — Devotional compositions performed with lyrical grace
  • Costume & Tradition — The iconic white-and-gold kasavu and Kerala jewellery
  • Stage Performances — Regular recitals and cultural event participation

EXCLUSIVE TO BHARATANATYAM

Certificate
Course

Our structured Certificate Programme in Bharatanatyam is a formally recognised pathway for dedicated students who wish to earn credentials in classical dance. Each level is assessed by qualified examiners and a certificate is awarded upon successful completion.

Level 01 Prathamika — Foundation

Basic adavus, posture training, rhythmic awareness and introductory jathis. Open to all beginners from age 6.

🕑 1 Year 🎓 Examination + Certificate
Level 02 Madhyamika — Intermediate

Advanced adavus, Alarippu, Jatiswaram, basic abhinaya and Nritta compositions. Requires Level 01 certification.

🕑 2 Years 🎓 Examination + Certificate
Level 03 Visharda — Advanced

Complete Margam repertoire, Varnam, Padam, Tillana and full Abhinaya. Culminates in Arangetram eligibility.

🕑 2–3 Years 🎓 Grand Certificate + Recital

Certificate courses are available exclusively for Bharatanatyam. Students in Mohiniyattam may enquire about future certification plans.
